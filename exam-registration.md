# AWS Cloud Practitioner Exam Registration Guide (CLF-C02)

## Overview

This guide provides a quick, beginner-friendly process to register for the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner exam.

---

## Steps to Register

### 1. Access AWS Certification Portal

* AWS Certification Portal: https://aws.amazon.com/certification/
* Schedule Exam: https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certification-prep/
* Sign in: https://www.aws.training/
* CertMetrics Dashboard: https://www.aws.training/Certification

---

### 2. Select Exam

* Exam Details (CLF-C02): https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-cloud-practitioner/

---

### 3. Choose Exam Mode

* Exam Provider (Pearson VUE): https://home.pearsonvue.com/aws

**Online Requirements:**

* System Test: https://home.pearsonvue.com/aws/onvue
* Exam Policies: https://home.pearsonvue.com/aws/onvue-policies

---

### 4. Accept Policies

* ID Requirements: https://home.pearsonvue.com/Test-takers/OnVUE-online-proctoring/ID-requirements

---

### 5. Schedule Exam

* Scheduling Portal: https://www.aws.training/Certification

---

### 6. Payment

* Exam Cost Info: https://aws.amazon.com/certification/pricing/

---

### 7. Confirmation

* Manage Appointment: https://home.pearsonvue.com/aws

---

## Important Rules

* Exam Policies: https://home.pearsonvue.com/aws/onvue-policies

---

## Pro Tips

* Exam Readiness: https://aws.amazon.com/training/learn-about/cloud-practitioner/

## Summary

**Login → Select Exam → Choose Mode → Schedule → Pay → Confirm**

Focus on preparation and consistency to succeed.
